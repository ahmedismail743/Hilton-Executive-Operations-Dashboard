# Hilton Executive Operations Dashboard
An executive-level operational and revenue analysis dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. This project evaluates hotel booking performance, guest demographics, revenue distribution channels, and cancellation trends to support data-driven strategy in hospitality management.

# 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes 119,390 hotel bookings representing $42.72M in total revenue. The primary goal is to identify core growth drivers, key booking channels, guest segments, and operational risk areas like cancellation rates.

# 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Revenue: $42.72M

- Average Booking Value: $357.85

- Max Revenue (Single Booking): $7,590

- Total Bookings: 119,390

- Cancellation Rate: 37%

# 📈 Key Insights & Findings
1. Peak Seasonality:

   - August generated the highest monthly revenue, peaking at $7,944,717.

2. Customer Segmentation:

   - Couples account for 68% of total bookings, followed by Singles (19%) and Families (13%).

3. Distribution Channels:

  - Online Travel Agents (Online TA) serve as the top revenue generator, contributing $23,942,048 (over 55% of overall revenue).

4. Lead Time Dynamics:

 - Bookings with higher lead times drove the highest revenue segment at $21,260,107, indicating that early planners contribute significantly higher value.

5. Operational Risk:

 - A 37% overall cancellation rate points to potential revenue loss and highlights a key area for policy optimization and targeted retention strategies.

# 🛠️ Tools & Skills Applied
- Data Modeling & Structuring: Organizing raw operational data into structured tables.

- Aggregations & Analytics: Excel Formulas, Pivot Tables, and statistical measures.

- Dashboard Design: Custom layout, KPI cards, visual hierarchy, interactive slicers (Hotel Type, Customer Type, Years).
